Give your children the gift of Ethiopian culture, history, and language! Habesha Adventure (የኢትዮጵያ ጉዞ) is an interactive educational game designed to help children explore iconic Ethiopian landmarks—such as Fasil Ghebbi (Gondar Castle) and traditional Gojo homes—while practicing both Amharic and English. ✨ Key Features: - 🌐 Bilingual Mode: Switch instantly between Amharic (አማርኛ) and English. - 🏰 Cultural Exploration: Claim historic Ethiopian landmarks and roles. - 🎁 Shareable Gift Card: Perfect for birthdays, holidays, and diaspora families. - 📱 Instant Play: Runs directly in any mobile or tablet web browser—no app store download required!
